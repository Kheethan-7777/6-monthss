For You

Hey baby,

I hope you're well.

You know, if someone had asked me this time last year if I would be in a relationship now, I probably would've laughed and said no, because at that point, I didn't think I deserved to be in one. And now, here I am, writing this letter to you.

I'll never forget the day I followed you on Instagram. I remember snatching my boy's phone from his hand just to send your Instagram to myself, and it didn't even take long for you to follow me back. You must've been excited when I followed you. 😂 But one of the best days of my life was when we started talking on the 7th of December.

To be honest with you, I knew exactly what I was doing when I replied to your story. That day, I didn't want to wait anymore because I had been wanting to talk to you for a while, but I didn't want to rush it. And then, when I finally DM'd you, you replied in less than an hour.

Ever since then, we've been talking every day, right up until now. And yes, we've had our ups and downs, but there is no one in this world I would rather go through all of that with than you.

You bring out the best in me in ways I never could have imagined, and I am so grateful to have you in my life. You are the best girlfriend I could ever ask for, and every day I thank God for blessing me with an amazing woman like you.

You are smart, kind, beautiful, caring, and a little funny. (I'm joking, you're very funny. 😂)

I can't wait to spend the rest of my life with you, and I know for certain that you are the person I want to spend the rest of my life with. There are very few things I am certain about in life, and one of those things is you.

I love you so much, Lex. You are everything I have ever needed and the best thing that has happened to me in I don't even know how long. I promise I will never leave you, my sweet girl.

I can't wait to see what the future holds for us and to build so many more memories together. I love you, and I miss you so much, my beautiful princess.

Kheethan Fitz
